White Rice

Description

The clasic white rice dish perfect company to any dish

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white rice
  • 2 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Rince the rice in water until clean
  2. In a saucepan with a lid, combine the rinced rice, water, salt, and olive oil
  3. Boil over medium heat and stir briefly to ensure the rice is evenly distributed
  4. Simmer, reduce heat and let it simmer undisturbed for 15 minutes
  5. Rest for 10 minutes
